# Overhead Privacy Policy

Updated October 5, 2026

Overhead uses your location to show aircraft near you. This policy describes what leaves your iPhone and what stays on it.

## Location

When you allow location, Overhead sends your precise coordinates to airplanes.live and adsb.lol so it can load aircraft within about 50 kilometers. The Home Screen widget uses the same request for the nearest flight. Coordinates are not linked to your name, and they are not used to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

## Camera

The scanner uses the camera only while it is open, so you can point at an aircraft. Overhead does not save or upload those frames.

## On this iPhone

Your display name, profile photo, and spotted aircraft stay in the app’s storage on this device. Overhead does not upload them and does not require an account.

## Airline marks

To show a carrier’s mark, Overhead may request an image using the airline code from Google Flights or Kiwi. That request does not include your location or name.

## Route details

When route lookup is available, Overhead may send an aircraft’s transponder address — not your name or location — to OpenSky or AeroDataBox for departure, arrival, and airframe details. The map works without those services.
