# Overhead — Recording script (~2 min)

Open this on your phone while you screen-record. **Say** the left column. **Do** the right column. One continuous take is fine.

---

| **Say** | **Do** |
|---------|--------|
| You hear it before you see it. You look up. There's a plane overhead, and you still don't know what it is. | Map open, planes moving. Don't tap yet. |
| My friends and I plane-watch for fun. We compete to name the model first. Right now we still Google it while the plane's already passing. | Same map. |
| I built Overhead to fix that. It runs on my iPhone and Apple Watch, with live flight data. This is the real app. | Quick flash of your home screen or app icon, then back to the map. |
| Watch. | Tap the closest plane. Quick card comes up. |
| I left the map empty on purpose so the sky stays the focus. This card is glass, so it still feels like you're outside. | Hold the quick card. |
| The callsign uses an LED dot matrix, like an airport board. Coral for the airline. The numbers line up in one column: altitude, speed, heading. This is what we'd be arguing about. | Point at callsign, airline, then the rows. |
| The actual aircraft type and registration are one level deeper. I used a long press so you don't open specs by accident. Hold "See Airplane Details." | Long-press the button, release into the detail sheet. |
| The sheet only covers part of the screen so you never lose the map. Here's the model and registration we used to search for manually. | Show the Type and Registration rows. Map still visible at the top. |
| When you're not even in the app, the widget shows the nearest flight to you. Tap it, and you're on that plane instantly. Same game with my friends, no hunting on the map. | Go to Home Screen, tap the Overhead Board widget, app opens on that flight. |
| On the Watch, same LED look for a quick glance. *(skip this line if you're out of time)* | Show the Watch app for two seconds. *(optional)* |
| I explored the visuals with AI tools, then shipped this in SwiftUI. I'm applying to Shopify's Design Apprentice Program in Product Design because I like designing for moments that already happen, like a plane over your head. Thanks for watching. | End card: **Overhead** · Design Apprentice, Product Design · your name |

---

## If you only remember three beats

1. **Hook** → plane overhead, don't know what it is.  
2. **Tap** → quick card answers; **long-press** → model/reg.  
3. **Widget tap** → nearest plane, app opens.

---

## Widget won't load?

Open the app once until flights appear, go Home, then record the widget tap.
